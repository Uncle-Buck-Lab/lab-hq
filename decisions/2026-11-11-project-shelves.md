# Project shelves and Kit interest

Made Nov 11, 2026, in the planning Project. This is the lab's future-state structure, not a launch plan.

| Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| Lab projects sit on five shelves: Reports and deep dives (to read), Dashboards and tools (to interact with), Agentic brands (characters that publish from data connectors), Blog (the founder's own voice, including an AI basics series) and Kits (paid build-your-own files) | Visitors come for different things; the shelves say what kind of thing each project is |
| A shelf appears on the site only once it holds live work | Consistent with showing live work only; no empty shelves |
| Experiments are a tag, not a shelf. A project or post tagged as an experiment shows up on an experiment log page | Every experiment is already something else (a brand, a tool, a blog series); a tag tracks them without a sixth thing to maintain |
| Kits are the lab's main planned revenue line; everything on the other shelves stays free, except the AI Money Report's later paid tier | The free work builds the audience and the proof; Kits sell the method |
| A stock-picker experiment, if it goes ahead, needs its own written rules on holdings, disclosure and advice before it runs | Public picks carry investment-advice and conflict risk beyond the current money rules |
| Kit interest is collected from launch as a tag on the lab dispatch list in beehiiv: a "tell me when Kits are available" option on the signup form, mentioned on the About page | Shows real demand from day one without selling anything at launch |
| Replaces the 250-person Kit trigger in `MONEY_RULES.md`. A kit is released when it is complete and tested and the founder judges the expressed interest is there, recorded as a dated decision per kit that cites the interest numbers | The founder wants to release on demand, not on a fixed count. The tested requirement and a written decision keep each release deliberate |
