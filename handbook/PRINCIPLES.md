# Principles

Published on the lab site. Every project follows all seven.

1. **Show the work.** Every claim has a source. Methods are public.
2. **Disclose the machine.** Every project page carries a lab notebook: hours, AI spend, what the agents did, what the founder reviewed and approved, and what broke.
3. **Name the downsides.** Cover AI's costs (jobs, energy, concentration of power, errors) alongside its wins.
4. **Same standard for everyone.** Bipartisan impartiality. Documented conduct only. Fact, stated claim and the lab's own read are labelled apart.
5. **A human approves anything public.**
6. **Credit people.** Artists, photographers, data sources, other writers. Pay for creative work where possible.
7. **Light footprint.** Scheduled jobs over always-on compute, and the smallest model that does the job well. Report what the lab spends on AI.

## Standing guardrails

- **Fully separate from the day job.** No data, clients or internal knowledge from the founder's employer in any lab work.
- **Political work holds to strict impartiality.** Congress Cards applies one standard to every member and every party, takes no partisan money and runs no ads.
- **Trust rules are lab-wide.** [TRUST_RULES.md](TRUST_RULES.md) applies to every publication, not only the report it started in.
