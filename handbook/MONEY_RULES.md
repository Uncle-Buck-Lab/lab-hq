# Money rules

Nothing is for sale at launch. The lab goes public to build an audience and a track record; money comes later, on triggers set in advance. These rules are published on the Principles page before the first dollar arrives.

## The rules

1. Every paid relationship is disclosed where it appears.
2. A sponsor never buys coverage, and a company the AI Money Report covers can't sponsor it.
3. The founder discloses holdings in any company the AI Money Report covers and doesn't trade it from 7 days before to 7 days after each issue.
4. Congress Cards takes no money from parties, campaigns, PACs or advocacy groups, and runs no ads.
5. Reader data is never sold or shared.
6. Uncle Buck only promotes brands he'd use anyway.

Uncle Buck says it this way: *"Nobody can pay me to change what the data says. But if a brand I already love wants to back the lab, I'm very buyable, and I'll tell you every time."*

## What's free and what's paid

| What | Free and public | Paid, later |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public-good projects (Congress Cards, Denver Sales) | The whole project | Nothing |
| AI Money Report | Every issue | Possibly a deeper tier: full data, alerts, archive |
| How it was built | The lab notebook: hours, spend, what the agents did | **The Kit:** the MD files, templates, skills and playbooks to build the same thing |
| Uncle Buck's attention | Newsletter, site, social | Sponsorships, brand partnerships, consulting, speaking |

## When to start charging

Set now so the decision isn't made on a whim. The founder can move a trigger, but only by writing a dated decision.

| Stream | Starts when |
| --- | --- |
| The Kit | A kit is complete and tested, and the founder judges there is enough interest from the Kit interest tag on the lab dispatch list. Each release gets a dated decision citing the numbers (see `decisions/2026-11-11-project-shelves.md`) |
| Newsletter sponsorships | 2,500 subscribers with healthy open rates |
| Brand partnerships | A brand the founder already likes asks, or the audience justifies pitching one |
| Display ads | 25,000 monthly pageviews |

## Opportunities

Partnerships, consulting, speaking and job offers that come from the public work are logged in `lab-desk/inbound.md`, including the ones declined. Once money starts, revenue per focus hour goes into the registry beside each project's hours.
