# LLC timing

Made Nov 9, 2026, in the planning Project.

| Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| The Washington LLC is formed in about two months (early January 2027), alongside the Congress Cards build | Congress Cards carries the most legal exposure of any lab project, and the free launch doesn't need an entity. Pairing them sets a real date |
| The LLC and media liability insurance are both in place before any Congress Cards profile is published | The entity protects the founder's personal assets; the insurance covers claims over what's published, which the LLC alone doesn't |
| If money comes in or an agreement is signed before then, the LLC is formed first | Neither should run through the founder's personal accounts |
| The founder reviews his employment agreement's outside-work and IP-ownership terms now | Who owns the work is a separate question from the entity, and it gets harder to answer later |
| Lab spending is tracked separately from personal spending starting now | Clean records now, and those costs can count as startup costs once the LLC exists |
