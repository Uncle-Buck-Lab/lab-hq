## Shipping a change

1. Branch, change, test locally.
2. Open a PR; the approval tier decides who reviews it (see the lab's GOVERNANCE.md).
3. After merge, confirm the Action succeeded and read its notices. Nothing is live until both the run and the Pages deploy succeed.
