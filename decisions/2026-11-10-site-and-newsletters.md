# Site and newsletters

Made Nov 10, 2026, in the planning Project.

| Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| The lab presents as a creative research lab: research, analysis, building and learning in public. Making money is not the site's job at launch | That's what the lab is for. Revenue comes later, from the work |
| Uncle Buck appears as the lab's owner (About page, signature), not as the face driving content | The lab and its work lead; the persona is the name on the door |
| The main signup is the lab dispatch: lab news, the week's top DorkGraphs chart, project progress, and what was learned. Every dispatch carries at least one real piece of work | A dispatch is worth reading only if it has substance, not just status |
| The AI Money Report is its own list: free first, with a paid tier later | A paid tier needs its own subscriber list; splitting now avoids a migration |
| Email on beehiiv; the site on Astro and Cloudflare Pages, with a preview link on every pull request; Cloudflare Web Analytics | Free to run, maintainable by agents through pull requests, and the lists stay exportable |
| Project pages are generated from `registry/projects.yaml` | The site, Lab Desk and the project list can't drift apart |
| Launch pages: home, About, project pages, legal | Small enough to ship; the rest is added based on what visitors use |
| The site meets WCAG AA | Built in from the start, and a standard the lab can name publicly |
| A PO box or virtual mailbox goes in every email, before the first send | Required by law, and it keeps the founder's home address private |
