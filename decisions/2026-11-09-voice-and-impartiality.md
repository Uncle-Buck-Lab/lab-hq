# Uncle Buck's voice and impartiality

Made Nov 9, 2026, in the planning Project.

| Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| Uncle Buck's voice runs through every lab project | The persona is the brand, and the lab speaks as one person |
| The lab's lens is "follow the money, ask who benefits," applied to every side equally | It's the founder's real stance, and a lens used evenly is how the voice stays fair |
| Three layers: numbers never carry voice; explanations always do; opinion is labeled as opinion | Readers on any side can trust the data even when they disagree with the take |
| Congress Cards profiles carry no opinion; the voice appears only in the site copy and explainers around them | It keeps the project's bipartisan rule |
| Every piece passes the swap test before publishing: would we write the same sentence if the other side did it? | Catches bias before it ships |
| Punch up, never down: aim at politicians, executives, institutions and ideas, never at voters or everyday groups | The bridge only works if both sides stay in the room |
